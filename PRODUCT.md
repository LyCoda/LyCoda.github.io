# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Medtech and robotics employers evaluating Nate Chu's engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Present Nate's biomedical engineering background, project evidence, education and industry experience, and make contact and the CV easy to find.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing static HTML, CSS and JavaScript site hosted at LyCoda.github.io on GitHub Pages. Preserve the homepage sections, project URLs, contact details, CV and portrait reveal. Rebuild the styling and project reading experience. User delegates project presentation choices.

## Brand Commitments

Nate Chu. User requests a light, minimal, technical appearance, improved typography and less UI clutter. Retain existing hero imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

Original repository content plus supplied fNIRS dissertation, robotic arm presentation summary, OsteoTrack group report and personal reflection, REDCap blank form and scoring notes. Describe personal and group contributions accurately. Separate concepts from tested results and established instruments from custom assessments. Do not invent employment, performance, deployment or clinical validation claims.

## Product Principles

Let engineering evidence lead. Make case studies readable and easy to scan. Preserve detailed context through layered reading and source references. Keep functions accessible with keyboard, touch and reduced motion.
