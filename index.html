About

Engineering, with people in mind.

Curious about the signal.
Careful about what it means.

I’m Nate, a biomedical engineer from Hong Kong, with a first-class engineering degree from City St George’s, University of London, and an MSc in Medical Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from UCL.

My work spans physiological signals, embedded systems and medical-device development. I enjoy the point where an idea has to become something testable: a control circuit, an analysis pipeline or a workflow that someone else can use.

Alongside the technical work, I’m interested in how devices reach clinical practice—through clear evidence, usable systems and a realistic understanding of the people adopting them.
