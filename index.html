About

Engineering, with people in mind.

I’m Nate, a biomedical engineer from Hong Kong with a first-class BEng from City St George’s, University of London, and an MSc in Medical Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from UCL.

I work where physiological signals, embedded hardware and medical devices meet: the point where an idea has to become something testable, whether that is a control circuit, an analysis pipeline or a clinical workflow that someone else can run.

  3. 03
